Right now only Block 34 route is added. Other routes will be added later. For Block 34 click here.









Route to Block 34

Start from the Main Gate. Go straight on the main road for some time and you will see the ground. Go past the ground and then turn right near the food court. After that keep walking and you will reach Block 34. It takes some time to reach. When you reach the block find the entrance and go inside. Then go to your floor.
