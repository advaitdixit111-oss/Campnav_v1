Route finder Dashboard Feedback

User feedback on Version 1

Important: this feedback is sample (imaginary) feedback written to show how we would improve the prototype. Before submission, replace it with what real users say in our test sessions, and do not present it as real results.

Task given (neutral)

"You are at the Main Gate and you need to get to Block 34. Use this page to find your way and tell us what you are thinking as you go."

Feedback from 5 sample users

UserWhat happenedWhat they said
1. First-year studentScrolled up and down twice before finding the route."There is too much writing at the start. I did not know where to begin."
2. Parent of a new studentCould not read the route paragraph on a phone."The text is very small and the yellow background hurts my eyes."
3. Day scholarRead the table, still unsure where to turn."It says 'Right' but not how far to walk. How do I know I reached the food court?"
4. Senior studentUnderstood quickly but said freshers would not."Put each step on its own line with a number, and tell people what they should see."
5. Campus visitorClicked the "here" link and could not get back to the top."No back button and no help if I get lost. Also, how long will it take?"

What we will change in Version 2

  1. Cut the long introduction, so the route is the first thing users see.
  2. Show the route one numbered step at a time, with Next and Back buttons.
  3. Use larger text, a plain white background and strong colours so it is readable on a phone.
  4. Add "You should see..." under each step so users can check they are on track.
  5. Show the total walking time and "Step 2 of 6" so users know how far they are.
  6. Add a "Lost? Get help" box and a way to start over.
